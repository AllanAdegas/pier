Pier is **source-available**: Apache-2.0 with the *Commons Clause* condition and the Licensor's clarifications. This page sums up the [LICENSE](LICENSE) in plain words; when in doubt, the LICENSE prevails.

## Allowed, without asking or paying

- Using Pier in your company, including for commercial purposes: deploying your own systems, websites and services.
- Using Pier as an internal tool to host and deploy the systems you build for your customers, as long as they do not receive access to Pier as part of what they pay for.
- Studying, modifying, forking and sharing it free of charge, keeping the LICENSE and the NOTICE.
- Contributing to the project.

## Requires a commercial license (with royalties)

Any way of **selling** Pier, or something whose value comes entirely or substantially from it, **even with only a few changes**:

- selling or licensing copies of Pier or of a product based on it;
- offering Pier under another brand (*white label*);
- selling access to the panel as a hosted or managed service (PaaS, a "deploy panel" for customers);
- charging for installation, support or consulting whose subject is Pier itself.
